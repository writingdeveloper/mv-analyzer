# Dataset licensing and third-party rights

The public release dataset contains selected YouTube identifiers/metadata and
MV Analyzer's derived measurements and annotations. It does **not** include video,
audio, thumbnails, lyric transcripts, subtitle text, cookies, signed media URLs,
or raw model prompts/responses.

To the extent the project owns copyright or database rights in the selection,
arrangement, annotations, and derived measurements, those project-authored parts
are made available under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(CC BY 4.0)**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

This grant does **not** license third-party material or rights the project does not
own. Video IDs, titles, channel names, public counters, and other source metadata
remain subject to applicable third-party rights and platform terms. No rights are
granted to underlying music videos, recordings, artwork, thumbnails, lyrics, or
performances.

The dataset is an observational extreme-group research sample. It must not be
represented as a market-wide percentile dataset or a causal/success predictor.
See `dataset/README.md` and the methodology documents for sampling limitations.
